# Campaign Performance Benchmarks

## ROI Benchmarks by Tier

Based on existing partner campaign data:

| Tier | Expected ROI | Strong ROI | Weak ROI |
|------|-------------|-----------|---------|
| Nano | 3–6x | > 7x | < 2x |
| Micro | 4–8x | > 9x | < 3x |
| Mid | 3–7x | > 8x | < 2x |
| Macro | 3–6x | > 7x | < 2x |
| Mega | 2–5x | > 6x | < 1.5x |

## Conversion Rate Benchmarks

Conversion rate = promo redemptions / UTM clicks

| Deal Type | Expected Conv. Rate | Strong | Weak |
|-----------|-------------------|--------|------|
| Affiliate | 0.45–0.65 | > 0.70 | < 0.30 |
| Brand Ambassador | 0.40–0.60 | > 0.65 | < 0.25 |
| Paid Creative | 0.30–0.50 | > 0.60 | < 0.20 |

## CPA Benchmarks (Cost Per Acquisition)

CPA = total budget / total promo redemptions

| Tier | Good CPA | Acceptable | Expensive |
|------|---------|------------|-----------|
| Nano | < £5 | £5–15 | > £15 |
| Micro | < £8 | £8–20 | > £20 |
| Mid | < £12 | £12–30 | > £30 |
| Macro | < £20 | £20–50 | > £50 |

## Engagement Rate Benchmarks

| Tier | Good ER | Average | Poor |
|------|---------|---------|------|
| Nano | > 8% | 4–8% | < 4% |
| Micro | > 5% | 3–5% | < 3% |
| Mid | > 3% | 2–3% | < 2% |
| Macro | > 2% | 1–2% | < 1% |
| Mega | > 1% | 0.5–1% | < 0.5% |

## Top Performing Niches (Historical)

Based on affiliate and brand ambassador campaign data:

1. **Women's Fitness** — highest conversion rate, strong AOV
2. **Bodybuilding** — strong ROI, loyal purchasing audience
3. **Gym Lifestyle** — high reach, moderate conversion
4. **Weightlifting / Strength** — niche but high-intent audience
5. **Running & Cardio** — growing segment, lower AOV

## Top Performing Content Styles

1. **Try-On Haul** — highest direct conversion (viewers in purchase mindset)
2. **Workout Vlog** — strong brand association, authentic integration
3. **Tutorial** — high trust, product features shown in context
4. **Transformation** — high emotional engagement, strong purchase intent

## Renewal Criteria

A creator is recommended for renewal when:
- ROI > 4x AND
- Conversion rate > 0.40 AND
- Brand safety remained GREEN throughout campaign AND
- Audience feedback in comments remained positive

## Important Notes

- ROI is only meaningful for affiliate and brand_ambassador deals (UTM-tracked)
- Gift seeding ROI cannot be measured — value is brand awareness only
- Paid creative partnership ROI is partial — not all conversions are trackable
- Always compare ROI within the same deal type, never across deal types
