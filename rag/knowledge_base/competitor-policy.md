# Competitor & Conflict of Interest Policy

## Direct Competitors (Do Not Partner)

Creators who have active paid partnerships with these brands are ineligible:

| Brand | Category | Why it conflicts |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| Lululemon | Premium activewear | Direct product overlap |
| Fabletics | Subscription activewear | Direct product overlap |
| Alphalete | Fitness apparel | Direct overlap, similar audience |
| NVGTN | Women's activewear | Direct overlap, same target demographic |
| Pursue Fitness | UK fitness apparel | Direct UK market competitor |
| Born Primitive | Strength apparel | Crossfit/powerlifting overlap |
| Gym Shark clones | Any knockoff brand | Brand dilution risk |

## Adjacent Brands (Assess Case-by-Case)

These brands are not direct competitors but require review before partnering:

- Nike, Adidas, Under Armour — large general athletic brands; allowed if Gymshark is the primary fitness apparel partner
- Protein supplement brands — not apparel, generally fine
- Sports equipment brands — not apparel, fine
- General fashion brands — fine if fitness content remains primary

## How Competitor Loyalty is Detected

The discovery pipeline flags a creator as `competitor_loyalty = True` when:
1. Transcript or title analysis finds competitor brand names with promotional language
2. Comment analysis reveals audience association with a competitor brand
3. LLM analysis identifies the creator as an active paid partner of a competitor

## What Counts as "Active Paid Partnership"

- Creator is listed as brand ambassador or affiliate on competitor website
- Creator uses a competitor promo code in recent videos (last 6 months)
- Creator has posted sponsored content for a competitor in last 6 months

## What Does NOT Count as Competitor Loyalty

- Creator has worn competitor gear in non-sponsored content
- Creator has mentioned a competitor positively in passing
- Creator was sponsored by a competitor more than 12 months ago with no renewal

## Exclusivity Clauses

Brand Ambassador and Co-Created Collection deals include:
- **Category exclusivity:** Creator cannot work with direct competitors during contract period
- **Duration:** Exclusivity applies for contract duration + 3 months post-contract

Affiliate and Paid Creative deals:
- No exclusivity by default
- Exclusivity can be negotiated for an additional 15% fee

## Reporting Conflicts

If a partner is found to be in breach of competitor exclusivity:
1. Partnerships manager notified within 24 hours
2. Creator contacted within 48 hours to discuss
3. If unresolved within 7 days, contract legal review initiated
