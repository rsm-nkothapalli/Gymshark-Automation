# Gymshark Brand Voice & Guidelines

## Brand Identity

Gymshark is a community-led fitness apparel brand founded in 2012. We are not just a clothing company — we are a movement built by and for the fitness community. Our brand is defined by authenticity, ambition, and belonging.

## Tone of Voice

- **Energetic but not shouty.** We inspire, we don't hype.
- **Warm and peer-level.** We talk to creators like teammates, not vendors.
- **Direct and confident.** No filler words, no corporate jargon.
- **Inclusive.** Gymshark is for everyone who trains — not just elite athletes.

## Banned Phrases — Never Use

The following phrases are explicitly banned from all outreach and briefs:

- "amazing" — overused and hollow
- "love your content" — sounds copy-pasted
- "huge fan" — sycophantic
- "perfect fit" — cliché
- "came across your page" — impersonal
- "inspiring" — vague and overused
- "collab" as a noun — sounds informal in written comms
- "synergy" — corporate jargon

## What Gymshark Stands For

- Real training, real people
- Community over competition
- Functional performance gear that looks as good as it performs
- Sustainability and ethical manufacturing progress
- Diversity in fitness — all bodies, all backgrounds

## What Gymshark Does NOT Stand For

- Extreme diet culture or dangerous weight loss messaging
- Body shaming (any direction)
- Steroid use or performance-enhancing drug promotion
- Toxic masculinity or exclusionary messaging
- Clickbait or dishonest content

## Visual Identity

- Primary colour: Black (#000000) and white (#FFFFFF)
- Accent: Gymshark green (#B5FE00 in digital contexts)
- Logo: Clean shark fin mark — never distort or recolour
- Typography: Clean sans-serif, never decorative fonts in official comms
