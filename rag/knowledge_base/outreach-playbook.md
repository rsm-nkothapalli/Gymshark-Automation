# Creator Outreach Playbook

## Core Principles

1. **Be specific, not generic.** Reference something real about their content.
2. **Lead with value, not ask.** Tell them what they get before you ask anything.
3. **Match the energy of their tier.** Nano = warm and casual. Mega = brief and professional.
4. **Never lie or oversell.** Gymshark's reputation depends on honest partnerships.

## Tone by Tier

### Nano (< 10K subs)
- Tone: Warm, peer-level, conversational
- Length: Up to 300 words
- Ask: Gift with no strings attached
- Feel: Like a message from a fellow fitness fan who works at Gymshark

### Micro (10K–100K subs)
- Tone: Friendly but professional
- Length: Up to 280 words
- Ask: Affiliate partnership — earn commission on your promo code
- Feel: A genuine opportunity being offered, not a mass email

### Mid (100K–500K subs)
- Tone: Professional, respectful of their business
- Length: Up to 260 words
- Ask: Paid creative partnership or ambassador discussion
- Feel: Business proposal from a brand that knows their work

### Macro (500K–2M subs)
- Tone: Direct, concise, assumes they get many pitches
- Length: Up to 220 words
- Ask: Ambassador retainer or co-creation conversation
- Feel: Peer-level brand proposal

### Mega (2M+ subs)
- Tone: Brief, pitch-led, respects their time
- Length: Up to 200 words
- Ask: Strategic partnership or collection collab
- Feel: Executive-level outreach

## Message Structure

1. **Hook** — one specific observation about their content (not generic praise)
2. **Who we are** — one sentence only, assume they know Gymshark
3. **The offer** — what they get, be specific about the deal type
4. **The ask** — one clear call to action
5. **Sign-off** — name, title, Gymshark Partnerships

## What to Reference in the Hook

Good hooks reference:
- A specific video theme or series they do regularly
- Their niche authenticity ("the way you document real training progress")
- Their community ("your audience clearly trusts your product opinions")
- Their content style ("your try-on format gives really honest context")

Bad hooks:
- "I've been following you for a while" — unverifiable
- "Your content is amazing" — hollow
- "You'd be a perfect fit for Gymshark" — every brand says this

## Banned Phrases

Never use in any outreach message:
- "amazing", "inspiring", "love your content"
- "huge fan", "perfect fit", "came across your page"
- "collab opportunity", "synergy", "exciting partnership"
- Any claim about guaranteed earnings or exposure

## Channel-Specific Notes

- **YouTube:** Lead with their video content. Reference workout style or series.
- **Instagram:** Reference their visual aesthetic or Reel format.
- **TikTok:** Reference their format or trending sounds they use.

## Follow-Up Policy

- Wait 5 business days before following up
- One follow-up maximum per outreach cycle
- If no response after follow-up, mark as cold in CRM and revisit next quarter
