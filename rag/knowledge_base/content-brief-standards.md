# Creative Content Brief Standards

## What a Brief Must Include

Every product brief sent to a creator must contain:

1. **KEY MESSAGE** — One sentence: what Gymshark wants the audience to feel or do
2. **CONTENT FORMAT** — Specific suggestion: e.g. "YouTube workout integration, Instagram try-on Reel"
3. **MUST INCLUDE** — Exactly two brand points they must mention
4. **AVOID** — One specific thing: a tone, claim, or comparison to avoid
5. **POSTING WINDOW** — Best time to post based on niche and audience timezone

## Brief Quality Standards

### Good KEY MESSAGE examples
- "Show your audience that training hard and looking good aren't mutually exclusive."
- "Let your community see how Gymshark's [PRODUCT LINE] performs in a real [NICHE] session."

### Bad KEY MESSAGE examples
- "Promote Gymshark." — Too vague
- "Tell your audience to buy Gymshark." — Too sales-focused
- "Gymshark is the best gym brand." — Overclaims

### MUST INCLUDE guidelines
Always include:
- One product-specific point (fit, feel, material, or specific item name)
- One brand-level point (Gymshark quality, community, or specific campaign message)

Never mandate:
- Specific scripts or exact wording
- Claims about performance enhancement
- Comparisons to competitors

### AVOID guidelines
Be specific. "Don't be inauthentic" is not useful. Instead:
- "Avoid filming in unflattering lighting that doesn't represent the product fairly"
- "Don't make claims about specific calorie burn or weight loss"
- "Avoid mentioning competitor brands even negatively"

## Format Recommendations by Content Style

| Creator Style | Recommended Format |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Workout Vlog | Integrate product naturally mid-workout; no scripted segment |
| Try-On Haul | Dedicated try-on with honest commentary on fit |
| Tutorial | Feature product during the exercise being taught |
| Transformation | Show product as part of the journey, not a magic solution |
| Day-in-Life | Wear product throughout the day, mention naturally |

## Posting Window Guidelines

| Audience | Best Days | Best Times |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| General fitness | Tue–Thu | 6–9 AM, 6–9 PM local |
| Bodybuilding | Mon, Wed, Fri | 7–10 AM local (pre-workout) |
| Women's fitness | Wed–Sat | 8–11 AM local |
| Running/Cardio | Sat–Sun | 7–10 AM local |
| Yoga/Mindfulness | Sun–Tue | 7–9 AM local |

## Gift Seeding Brief Notes

For gift seeding deals, briefs should:
- Make clear there is NO posting obligation
- Still suggest what they could post if they want to
- Not include mandatory must-include or avoid sections — these are guidance only
- Feel like a personal note, not a contract

## What NOT to Put in a Brief

- Specific hashtags to use (creators know their audience better)
- Mandated caption length
- Demands to tag specific Gymshark accounts (can suggest)
- Any language implying the partnership is conditional on posting
