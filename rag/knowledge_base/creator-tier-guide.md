# Creator Tier Guide & GFS Thresholds

## Tier Definitions

| Tier | Subscriber Range | Typical Deal | GFS Minimum |
|------|-----------------|-------------|-------------|
| Nano | < 10,000 | Gift Seeding | 5.0 |
| Micro | 10,000 – 100,000 | Affiliate | 5.5 |
| Mid | 100,000 – 500,000 | Paid Creative / Ambassador | 6.0 |
| Macro | 500,000 – 2,000,000 | Brand Ambassador | 6.5 |
| Mega | 2,000,000+ | Co-Created Collection | 7.5 |

## Gymshark Fit Score (GFS) Formula

```
GFS = (authenticity × 0.40)
    + (comment_sentiment × 0.30)
    + (niche_alignment × 0.20)
    + (engagement × 0.10)
    + niche_bonus
    + content_style_bonus
    - penalties
```

### Score Components (all rated 1–10)

- **Authenticity (40%)** — Does content feel genuine? No scripted promos, honest product reactions, real training footage.
- **Comment Sentiment (30%)** — Are audience comments positive and engaged? Measured via VADER NLP.
- **Niche Alignment (20%)** — How well does the creator's content align with Gymshark's fitness positioning?
- **Engagement (10%)** — Views/subscribers ratio as a proxy for audience engagement rate.

### Penalties (subtracted from GFS)

- **Clickbait detected:** −1.5 points
- **Brand safety RED signals:** −2.0 points
- **Competitor loyalty:** −1.0 point
- **Authenticity negative signals:** −0.5 points

### Bonuses (added to GFS)

- **Niche bonus:** Up to +0.8 based on historical conversion data from top-performing niches
- **Content style bonus:** Up to +0.8 based on content styles that drive highest ROI

## Disqualification Rules

A creator is automatically disqualified regardless of GFS if:
- Brand safety = RED
- Competitor loyalty = True
- Clickbait detected = True

## Outreach Recommendation Rules

Outreach is recommended when ALL of the following are true:
- GFS ≥ tier minimum threshold
- Authenticity score ≥ 5
- Brand safety ≠ RED
- No clickbait detected
- No competitor loyalty

## Nano Creator Notes

Nano creators often have lower raw scores but higher genuine engagement rates. A nano creator with GFS 5.5 and 15% engagement rate often outperforms a micro creator with GFS 6.0 and 3% engagement on a per-£ basis for gift seeding campaigns.
