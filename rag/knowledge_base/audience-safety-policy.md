# Audience Safety & Brand Risk Policy

## Overview

Gymshark operates a two-layer safety check on all creator prospects:

1. **Content-level check** (Phase 0) — automated keyword scanning of video titles and transcripts during discovery
2. **Audience-level check** (Phase 1b, Skill 02) — VADER sentiment analysis of YouTube comments

Both must pass before outreach is approved.

## Brand Safety Labels

### GREEN — Safe to Proceed
- No safety-negative keywords detected in content
- Comment sentiment score ≥ 0.65
- Flagged comment percentage < 8%

### AMBER — Human Review Required
- Body image concern keywords present > 5% of comments, OR
- Flagged comment percentage ≥ 8% but ≤ 15%
- A human partnerships manager must review before outreach

### RED — Do Not Proceed
- Toxicity in comments > 5%, OR
- Flagged comment percentage > 15%, OR
- Creator has posted content promoting dangerous diet practices, drug use, or hate speech
- RED is permanent for the current pipeline cycle — do not retry

### UNKNOWN — Expand Sample
- Fewer than 50 comments available for analysis
- Run comment collection again with a larger sample before making a decision

## Safety-Negative Content Keywords

The following keyword categories in video titles or transcripts trigger AMBER or RED flags:

- **Extreme diet:** "1000 calorie", "starvation diet", "crash diet", "extreme cut"
- **Drug use:** "steroids", "sarms", "peptides", "gear" (in bodybuilding context)
- **Hate speech:** Any discriminatory language targeting protected groups
- **Dangerous training:** Content promoting injury-risking practices without safety caveats
- **Competitor promotion:** Overt endorsement of direct competitors (see competitor policy)

## Comment Sentiment Thresholds

| Metric | Threshold | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Avg compound sentiment | < 0.40 | Flag for review |
| Negative creator flags | > 5% of comments | AMBER |
| Toxicity rate | > 5% | RED |
| Flagged comments | > 15% | RED |
| Purchase intent signals | 0 in 50+ comments | Note in report |

## Competitor Loyalty Definition

A creator is marked competitor_loyalty = True if their content shows:
- Paid promotion of a direct Gymshark competitor
- Wearing competitor-branded gear in majority of recent videos
- Explicit endorsement language for competitors

Direct competitors: Lululemon, Fabletics, Alphalete, NVGTN, Pursue Fitness, Born Primitive

## Override Policy

A RED safety flag can only be overridden by the Head of Partnerships with written justification. AMBER flags can be cleared by any senior partnerships manager after manual review.
